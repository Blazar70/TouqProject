## 3. Bugs (new)

**B22 — Medium — A fully credited visit goes back on the "Unpaid" list**
- Where: `0093_rebill_after_full_credit.sql:16-28` moves a fully credited bill's link from `appointment_id` to `credited_appointment_id`. `report_unpaid` (current version in `0069_invoices_outlive_pets.sql:44`) lists every completed visit with `not exists (… invoices i where i.appointment_id = a.id)`. That check doesn't know about the new column. `billed()` (`0074_money_roles.sql:75-81`) works the same way, so the "Bill visit" button comes back too.
- Scenario: a cat's visit is billed at 450 SAR. The doctor decides it should be free (goodwill, or a complaint), so the manager credits the whole bill. The bill is now `paid` and leaves the list, but the visit comes back as "visit · 450.00 remaining". The receptionist working the Unpaid list calls the owner to collect 450 SAR. The entry stays until someone removes the visit's services or bills it again.
- Why it happens: 0093 treats every full credit as "we'll bill again" (a wrong bill). It can't tell that apart from "this visit is free".
- Fix (S): in `report_unpaid`, also skip visits that have `credited_appointment_id = a.id`. Rebilling still works from the visit's "Bill visit" button. Better (M): after a full credit, ask "Bill this visit again / The visit is free". Only the first choice releases the link.

**B23 — Low (note, not a bug yet) — A pet moved away and back loses its own history in the export**
- `pets_owner_since` (`0092:13-24`) resets `owner_since` on every owner change. If Bella moves from owner A to B by mistake and is moved straight back, A's PDPL export (`ownerExport.ts:30-31`) now leaves out all of A's earlier invoices and waivers.
- This is rare, and it gives *less* data, not someone else's data. Fix only if it comes up: log transfers in a small table instead of a single timestamp.

I found no new security holes in tonight's diff. Here is what I checked:

- **0093:** the trigger runs inside the existing `refresh_invoice_status` (security definer). Clients still have no update right on `invoices` (0061 revoke).

**M1 — Medium (setup gap, not code) — Password reset emails won't reach clinic managers**

- Username staff (`…@staff.touq.invalid`) are not affected, because the login page already tells them to ask their manager. Managers and the platform admin use real emails, though.
- The error is shown raw, in English: `friendlyError.ts` has no line for "not authorized" or "rate limit".
- Fix: see research section M (custom SMTP). Also add two `KNOWN` lines in `friendlyError.ts`.

## 6. HCI / visual / usability ideas

**H30 — Make the 80 mm receipt readable on a thermal printer** (S)
- Problem: the new `RECEIPT_80MM_STYLES` (`PrintContext.tsx:250-261`) keeps the A4 colours. Thermal printers print only pure black dots. Light grey (`#eee` row lines, `#888` "printed on", `#666` labels) and the gold `#b8860b` header lines come out faint and dotted, or vanish. The body is 11px, and Arabic letters at 11px on a 203 dpi head are hard to read. Each bilingual label ("Tax invoice · فاتورة ضريبية", `bi()` at `:226`) is also squeezed onto one line about 72 mm wide, so it wraps in odd places.
- Who: receptionists handing receipts to owners, and anyone checking the VAT lines.
- Change: in the 80 mm styles, set `* { color: #000 !important; border-color: #000 !important }`, `font-size: 12px`, and give labels `display: block` so the Arabic sits under the English. Do a test print on a real printer.

**H31 — The dashboard doesn't reload itself, so the waiting timer goes stale** (S)
- Problem: `DashboardPage.tsx:133-136` ticks the clock every minute, but the list is only loaded on open or when you tap Refresh. There is no Realtime subscription or periodic reload. A pet the doctor took in 30 minutes ago still shows "waiting 45 min" in amber. A new check-in doesn't appear (the push alert helps only if push is on).
- Who: doctors and nurses who leave the dashboard open on a tablet.
- Change: in the same interval, call `load()` quietly every 2 minutes, and again on `visibilitychange` when the tab comes back. Later (M), use a Supabase Realtime subscription on `appointments` for the clinic.

**H32 — iPad: "Push notifications aren't supported" should say how to get them** (S)
- Problem: on iPad and iPhone, web push only works after Touq is added to the Home Screen and opened from that icon. In a Safari tab, `PushManager` doesn't exist, so `SettingsPage.tsx:771` says "not supported in this browser". That reads as "impossible", not as "install first". iPads are common at Saudi front desks.
- Who: reception and doctors on iPads, who miss check-in and status alerts.
- Change: if the device is iOS and `!matchMedia('(display-mode: standalone)').matches`, show three steps in both languages: Share → "Add to Home Screen" → open Touq from the icon → then turn on notifications.

**H33 — Translate password-reset errors** (S, do it with M1)
- Problem: `LoginPage.tsx:51` shows `error.message` from Supabase. `FormError` translates only the texts listed in `friendlyError.ts`, and "Email address not authorized" and "email rate limit exceeded" aren't listed, so an Arabic-speaking manager sees English.
- Change: add the two lines and map them to "Couldn't send the email. Try again in an hour or ask the Touq admin."

### M) Transactional email for Supabase Auth

What Touq sends today: only password-reset emails for managers and the admin (staff accounts are created already confirmed, `email_confirm: true`). That is very low volume, maybe tens per month even with 20 clinics.

| Option | What it is | Price (seen 2026-10-28, unverified) | Effort | Risk / lock-in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase default sender | Built in | Free, but **delivers only to your Supabase team**, and only a few per hour | None | Unusable for real clinics |
| **Resend** | Developer email API, with an official Supabase integration and SMTP | Free 3,000/month (100/day). Pro $20/month for 50k | S: paste the SMTP details into Supabase Auth → SMTP settings, and add 3 DNS records (SPF/DKIM) on your domain | Low: plain SMTP, easy to switch |
| Amazon SES | AWS bare email service | About $0.10 per 1,000, no free tier outside EC2 | M: AWS account, leaving the sandbox needs a request, DNS | Low cost, more setup. The UAE region (me-central-1) exists, but SES support there wasn't confirmed |
| Postmark | Known for good delivery | $15/month for 10k, trial only | S | Paying for volume Touq doesn't need |
| Brevo | EU email/SMS platform with a documented Supabase SMTP guide | Free tier (limits not checked tonight) | S | Its marketing features aren't needed |

Optional later step: Supabase's **Send Email Hook** lets an edge function send the auth email (for example via Resend), so you can send a bilingual Arabic/English reset email with the clinic's logo. Effort M. Do it only if the default templates bother users. Note that free-tier projects can no longer edit the templates (Supabase changelog, June 2026). That is another reason to use a custom SMTP or the hook.

Why it matters for Touq: a locked-out manager can't reset staff passwords either, so the whole clinic depends on that one email arriving. It also needs a real sending domain (for example `touq.sa`), which you'll want anyway.

**Recommendation (M):** before the pilot, buy or choose the domain and set up **Resend through Supabase custom SMTP** (free, no code). Also set a sensible Auth email rate limit in the dashboard and add the two `friendlyError` lines (H33). Skip SES and Postmark at this size.

### N) Web push delivery (what Touq uses for check-in and status alerts)

What Touq has: its own web push with a VAPID key (`push.ts`, `_shared` sender in edge functions). That costs nothing and needs no vendor.

Facts that matter:
- **iPhone/iPad:** push works only for a web app added to the Home Screen and opened from that icon (iOS 16.4+). The permission request must come from that installed app, after a tap. A Safari tab never gets push, and iOS has no automatic install prompt. This is why H32 matters.
- **Declarative Web Push** (Safari/iOS 18.4+, March 2025): the server sends JSON (title, body, link) and iOS shows it without running service-worker code. Sources say it is more reliable, because iOS can't penalise or drop a "silent" push. Touq's current payload already includes the title, body and icon, so supporting it is mostly a matter of message format. Effort S–M. Worth a look if iPad alerts turn out to be flaky.
- Developers report subscriptions on iOS sometimes disappear. Touq should re-check `hasActivePushSubscription` when the app opens and quietly re-subscribe if permission is still granted (it may already partly do this, `SettingsPage.tsx:111`).

| Option | Price (seen 2026-10-28, unverified) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Keep self-hosted VAPID push (today) | Free | Full control, no third party sees clinic data. Touq's own edge functions handle sending |
| OneSignal | Web push stays free. The new 1,000-MAU (monthly active users) cap from Sept/Oct 2026 applies to mobile app push. Growth $19/month + $0.012/MAU | Adds a third-party SDK, and pet names in notifications go through a US vendor (a PDPL transfer question) |

**Recommendation (N):** keep Touq's own push and don't add OneSignal. Do H32 (install instructions on iOS). Before the pilot, test one full check-in alert on a real iPad installed to the Home Screen. Put Declarative Web Push on the list for after the pilot.
