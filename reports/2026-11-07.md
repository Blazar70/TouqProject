## 3. Bugs (new)

**B48: Medium: the invoice search and date filter only look at the newest 300 invoices**
- Where: `src/pages/PaymentsPage.tsx:46` (`INVOICE_HISTORY_LIMIT = 300`), `:126` (`.limit(...)`), with the filters running only on rows already in the browser at `:411-428`. The message at `:807` says `payments.noneMatch`.
- Scenario: a clinic issues about 30 invoices a day. Three weeks later an owner comes back to dispute a bill. The receptionist types the invoice number, or picks a date range from three weeks ago, and sees "No invoices match". The invoice is in the database, but it was never loaded into the page. Credit notes and reprints for older bills then have to start from the pet's file, and nothing on screen says so.
- Fix (S–M): when search or a date filter is set, run the query on the server (`.ilike('invoice_number', …)`, `.gte/.lte('created_at', …)` using clinic-time bounds) instead of filtering the 300 loaded rows. At the very least, show "Showing the newest 300. Older invoices: open the pet's file."

**B49: Low (money display): "Card/Apple Pay today" includes payments from an owner's prepaid balance**
- Where: `src/pages/PaymentsPage.tsx:430-435`. The non-card amount is worked out as `nonCash = total - cash`, and the list of payments holds every method, `'balance'` included (`:119`). Compare `ReportsPage.tsx:120-133`, where the cash-up report correctly leaves `balance` out of the drawer.
- Scenario: an owner topped up 500 SAR last week. Today they use 200 SAR of that balance for a visit. The Payments page shows "Today's total" and "Card/Apple Pay today" both 200 SAR higher than the money that actually came in, so the receptionist's end-of-day check against the card machine's slip is off by 200 SAR. The cash-up report gives the right number, so two screens disagree.
- Fix (S): filter out `method === 'balance'` from the total and the card amount, or show it as its own tile, "From prepaid balance".

**B50: Low: the "today" tiles on Payments drift until the page is reloaded**
- Where: `src/pages/PaymentsPage.tsx:389` and `:869`.
- Scenario 1: a manager enters a payment and backdates it to yesterday 20:00 (allowed, 0066). `:389` still adds it to *today's* tiles on screen.
- Scenario 2: the common "pay later" case. Someone opens an existing invoice and adds a payment through `InvoiceDetail`. `onChanged` updates the invoice list, but the today tiles don't change. The tiles are wrong until the next reload.
- Fix (S): after either save, reload the small `todayQuery` (one query) instead of patching the numbers by hand.

**B51: Low: a staff reminder can be sent twice if two runs overlap**
- Where: `supabase/functions/send-appointment-reminders/index.ts:53-74`. The function checks `appointment_reminders_sent`, then sends the push, then inserts the row.
- Scenario: one run is slow (many staff, slow push service) and the next 15-minute run starts before it ends. Both see "not sent yet", so the doctor gets the same reminder twice. The insert from the second run then fails on the primary key (0023:32), and nobody notices. The inpatient dose reminders in the same file already avoid this by claiming the row first (`:96-101`).
- Fix (S): first `upsert(..., { onConflict: 'appointment_id,profile_id', ignoreDuplicates: true }).select()`, and skip the send when no row comes back, the same way the inpatient code does.

**Minor note, not a separate finding:** the invoice date filter (`PaymentsPage.tsx:413-414`) builds its day bounds from the **device's** time zone, while the rest of the app uses `clinicMidnight()`. That is fine on Saudi devices and wrong on a laptop set to another zone. Fold it into the B48 fix by using `clinicMidnight(dateFrom)`.

## 6. HCI / visual / usability ideas

**H74: Quick date buttons on the invoice list** (S)
- Problem: finding "yesterday's invoices" means opening two date pickers (`PaymentsPage.tsx:788-795`). On an iPad, the native date picker takes 4–6 taps each, and in Arabic it shows Western-style month names.
- Who: receptionists answering "what did I pay yesterday?", managers.
- Change: add a row of chips (small toggle buttons) above the filters: **Today · Yesterday · This week · This month**, which fill `dateFrom`/`dateTo` from `clinicDay()`. Keep the date pickers for custom ranges. Pairs with B48: the chips should search the server, not the newest 300.

**H75: Make the "today" tiles on Payments match the cash-up, and tap through to it** (S)
- Problem: Payments shows Today total / Cash / Card / Awaiting, and Reports shows the cash-up, but the two can disagree (B49, B50). There's also no link from one to the other.
- Who: receptionists closing the day, managers.
- Change: use the same rule (no `balance` in the drawer), add a small "From balance" line, and make the Cash and Card tiles links to `/reports` on today's cash-up. ~10 lines.

**H76: Say when an invoice search went past what's loaded** (S, if B48 isn't fixed fully)
- Problem: "No invoices match" sounds final, so staff think the invoice was never made.
- Change: when the loaded list is exactly 300 long and filters are on, the empty state says "Not in the newest 300 invoices. Try the pet's file." plus a **Search all** button that runs the server query.

**H77: An "insurance claim pack" print for an insured pet** (S–M, pairs with research AF)
- Problem: Saudi pet insurance (Tree) pays the owner back after the visit, and asks for the vet's itemised invoice, the full clinical history and proof of the microchip. Touq can already print each of these (`printHistory` with the microchip, `printInvoice`), but staff must print them one by one and the invoice doesn't show the diagnosis.
- Who: reception (owner asks "can you give me papers for my insurance?"), doctors.
- Change: one **Print for insurance** button on the invoice that prints the invoice with the visit's diagnosis line, then the pet's visit history, in one window. Optional `pets.insurer` text field later, only if clinics ask.

**H78: A post-visit "how was your visit?" message that follows Google's rules** (S–M, pairs with research AG)
- Problem: Saudi clinics compete on Google Maps ratings, and owners rarely review unless asked. Touq already sends owner reminders through WhatsApp links (`ownerReminders.ts`), but there is no thank-you message.
- Who: managers (clinic reputation), reception.
- Change: in the reminders list, add a "Thank you + review" item one day after a completed visit, with a bilingual text and the clinic's Google review link (a new `clinics.review_url` setting). Send it to **every** owner, not just the happy ones (see research AG for why).

### AF) Pet insurance in KSA: what a clinic needs to give the owner

| Item | What I found (seen 2026-11-07) |
|---|---|
| Who sells it | **Tree** (Tree Digital Insurance Agency), launched in 2024 and described in the press as the first pet insurance licensed by the Insurance Authority. Plans: Essential / Premium / Royal, and Accident Only or Accident & Illness. Dog plans include third-party liability. A report says Tawuniya is adding cat/dog cover through Tree; I couldn't confirm whether other insurers sell it. |
| Who can join | Pets aged 16 weeks or older, **microchipped**, with an annual health check by a licensed vet. |
| How claims work | **Owner pays the clinic, then claims within 30 days.** The money goes to the owner's bank account, not to the clinic. There is a co-payment of 20% (pet insured before 4 years old) or 30% otherwise. |
| What the vet provides | Original **itemised invoice**, **full clinical history** since the pet was registered at the clinic, the latest health check report, **proof of microchip**, and a vet-filled claim form (`.docx`, emailed to petclaims@tree.com.sa). The form's footer still says "Tawuniya", so it may be an old version. |
| Price | Not published; it depends on age, breed and plan. Get a quote. |
| API for clinics | None found. Claims go through the owner's app or by email. |

Why it matters for Touq: insurance doesn't change Touq's billing, because the clinic is paid in full as usual. But every insured owner will ask the clinic for a stack of papers. Touq already holds all of them (the microchip number, H42; visit history print; itemised VAT invoice). Producing them in one tap (H77) is a nice selling point at almost no cost, and the microchip requirement makes Touq's microchip field more valuable.

Risks: one small, young market with one main seller, and form and rules may change. Don't build an integration.

**Recommendation (AF):** **v2: nothing.** **v3: H77** (one "Print for insurance" button). Re-check in 2027 whether a second insurer or a direct-billing scheme (where the insurer pays the clinic) appears. Only then would an `insurer`/policy-number field on the pet be worth adding.

### AG) Collecting owner feedback and Google reviews

| Option | What it does | Price (seen 2026-11-07) | Saudi fit | Effort | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Google review link in Touq's own thank-you message** | Clinic pastes its review link (from Business Profile → "Ask for reviews", a `g.page` short link). Touq adds it to a bilingual WhatsApp message after the visit | Free | Google Maps is the main local search in KSA; WhatsApp is the main channel | **S**: one clinic setting, one reminder kind, reusing the existing WhatsApp-link flow | None. No API, no data leaves Touq |
| **Hand-built `search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=…` link** | Opens the review form directly | Free | Same | S | **Not in Google's documentation**, so it could stop working. Prefer the dashboard link |
| **Review-management SaaS** (Birdeye, Podium, local GBP agencies) | Sends requests, tracks ratings, replies | Usually monthly subscriptions; not checked tonight | Mostly US-focused | M (owner phone numbers go to a third party) | Personal data shared → PDPL (Saudi data-protection law) processor agreement needed; lock-in |
| **Touq's own 1–5 "how was your visit?" score** | Private score stored in Touq for the manager | Free | Good | M (a public page with no login, abuse protection, a new table) | Must not be used to filter who gets the Google link (see below) |

The key rule (Google Maps contribution policy): Google forbids businesses from **"discouraging or prohibiting negative reviews, or selectively soliciting positive reviews"**, and from paying or giving rewards for reviews. A secondary report says that since April 2026 Google also bans asking staff to collect a target number of reviews or reviews with specific wording. Breaking these rules can get reviews removed and the Business Profile restricted. Plain words: **Touq must send the review link to every owner, or to none.** A common "ask for a score first, and only send happy owners to Google" flow (called "review gating") is not allowed.

**Recommendation (AG):** **v3: H78.** Add a `review_url` clinic setting and a "thank you + review" item in the existing owner-reminders list, sent to all completed visits. Leave out a private score and any third-party tool until a clinic asks for one. Keep the message free of health details (pet name only), as the current reminders do.
